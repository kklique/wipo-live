In practice, this means that well-known works were overcounted and local and less famous authors were often not counted at all. AI helps us change this in three ways. First, music recognition. Each recording has a unique digital fingerprint.

Today the platform monitors more than 130 TV and radio channels around the clock. And from the 2027, it will cover more than 1,000 users across Kazakhstan.
