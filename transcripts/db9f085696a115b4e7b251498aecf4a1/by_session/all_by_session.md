The modern IP system, the patent system of and then the first corporate statutes may have been birthed during the Renaissance and Age of Enlightenment, but came into maturity during the industrial age, the 1880s.

Here at WIPO, for example, the WIPO Internet Treaties of the 1990s, which will celebrate their 30th anniversary in a few weeks' time, were a very important and rapid response to those digital transformations.
Much of this adaptation has involved adding layers onto an existing architecture whose basic categories were developed for an earlier industrial economy without having to rethink the architecture. AI is forcing us to turn our attention to the underlying architecture and to ask ourselves fundamental questions about its continued relevance.
