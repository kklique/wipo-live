Today, the world is at a major turning point driven by the rapid advancement of AI. In July of this year, the Government of Japan developed Japan's Growth Strategy, in addition to serving as the Commissioner of the JPL, I was responsible for its development as a member of Cabinet Secretariat. In the strategy, we have selected 17 strategic fields with a projected total scale of domestic, public and private investment expected to exceed 373 trillion yen or approximately 2.3 trillion yen.
US dollars, cumulatively by fiscal year 2040. Within this framework, the AI transformation position as an accelerator for growth.
Accordingly, several core fields essential for this transformation, including AI and semiconductors, have been prioritized. By the end of the year, we will formulate the Japan's growth strategy implementation plan, which will include the details and amounts of domestic investment over the next five years. leading up to around 2030. While the government of Japan positioned AI transformation as an accelerator of growth, the JPO is responding to the rapid development of AI by ensuring that our examination practice is future-ready, as well as reviewing our institutional frameworks.
In Japan, patent applications for AI-related inventions stood at approximately $ 8,000 in 2019, but exceeded $ 20 , 000 in 2024. To address the growth of these applications, the PL launched the Air Examination Support Team in 2021 to provide proper support for their examination. In addition, the JPL is promoting the use of AI and its operations and released the JPO AI vision this July.
The AI vision advocates a human centric approach as one of its key concepts aiming for the responsible use of AI. Through these ongoing efforts, the JPO has keenly reaffirmed the importance of deepening collaboration with stakeholders and taking into consideration trends in foreign countries regarding AI. As AI rapidly advances, it is essential for Member States and stakeholders to come together and share diverse experience.

This global platform truly responds to the demand of the times by providing a cross-cutting platform for the dialogue on IP and AI.
