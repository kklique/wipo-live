And will tell you a bit about a Danish bill that is proposed right now. currently regarding protection for all individuals against being defaked.
I think Anderson started by saying why do not we require authorization? for creation of deepfakes? And I think that is in line with the thought of the Danish government and parliament because what is being proposed is a protection for all individuals, whether you are an artist, a famous actor or just an everyday citizen. All people will be protected against deep fakes.
And the main purpose of the Act is to gain better enforcement. The protection of individuals of personality, has in almost all jurisdictions been based on case law regarding commercial use. And it is very difficult to have content taken down from social media platforms. For example, if you have to refer to case law from the'60s, 70s or 80s, we did not even have deepfakes then. So the main goal for the Danish legislation is to have a very clear provision.
