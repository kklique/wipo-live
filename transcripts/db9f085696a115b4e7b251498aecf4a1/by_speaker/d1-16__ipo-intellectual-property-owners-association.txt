Now I give the floor to Mohamed Ali, Member of IP Owners Association. The floor is yours. Thank you, Mr. Chair. I am making this statement on behalf of International Intellectual Property Owners Association, IPO, an international trade association whose membership includes over 125 companies. and spans over 30 countries. IPO represents a big tent of diverse companies, law firms, service providers and individuals in all industries and fields of technologies that own or are interested in Intellectual Property rights.
Part of IPO's mission is to promote predictable legal and IP systems which provide the certainty and stability that our members and all innovator needs to invest in innovative research and development to address society's greatest challenges. We truly appreciate hosting this event and the first global forum on IP and AI.
In order to promote consistent discussions about artificial intelligence and innovation, IPO and the American Intellectual Property Law Association have prepared category definitions for inventions related to artificial intelligence. as well as corresponding examples for each of the categories. Defining categories of inventions related to AI can provide a framework to facilitate continued work on AI policy issues by ensuring that everyone is using the same term in the same way.
