Another important issue is the protection of identity. AI can imitate our voices, faces and other personal traits. Non-authorized use harms, performance and creators as well as politicians. politicians, public figures and ordinary citizens. People must remain in control of how they likeness and identity are used. Clear rules and effective tools are needed.
The digital transformation will continue for many years and no country can address these challenges alone. I hope WIPO will continue its strong coordinating role. I wish you productive discussions and every success for this Forum. Again, I would like to congratulate WIPO on hosting the global forum on IP and AI and thank you, Director General, Daren Tang and the WIPO team for bringing everyone together to discuss an important issue of the AI era.
