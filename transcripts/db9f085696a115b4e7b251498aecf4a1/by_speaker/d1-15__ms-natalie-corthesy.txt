Similarly, Vietnam's new copyright decree makes it clear that content only gets corporate protection when humans have made a significant and decisive contribution to ensure that the final result accurately reflects their creative intent rather than the random or automatic arrangement of the aclarism. The draft registration guidelines released by the Philippines emphasize that works are eligible for registration only when they contain original expressions resulting from human author's creative contribution and meet the originality requirements.
Merely text prompts or instructions to AI should not by itself establish sufficient human creative contribution. Obviously, there is growing consensus in Asia that protecting content autonomously produced by AI will put the fundamental values in the principles of copyright law in crosshairs. so content entirely generated by AI cannot be protected by copyright, but AI can still serve as a meaningful tool for human authors to create works.
