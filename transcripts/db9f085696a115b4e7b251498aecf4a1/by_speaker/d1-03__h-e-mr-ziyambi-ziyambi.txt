We do not view AI merely as a novel technology. but as a catalyst for inclusive growth. enhanced public service delivery and economic diversification, while the state will fulfill its duty to provide robust regulatory direction and enabling legal environment, the true vanguard of this revolution will be the unyielding creativity of our people, nurtured within our universities and propelled by our private sector.
