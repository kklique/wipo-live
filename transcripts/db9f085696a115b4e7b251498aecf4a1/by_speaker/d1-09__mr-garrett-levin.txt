Back in 2019, WIPO's technology trends report on artificial intelligence was already telling a rather different story. Scientific publications in AI had been growing rapidly for decades and patent applications were following a similarly dramatic trajectory, with exponential growth in the years leading up to the report. The signals were there, but the question was, were we paying attention to them? And that raises a very interesting possibility.
