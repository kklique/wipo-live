Please imagine with me a small newspaper whose readers usually find its reporting through Google Search.
The publisher wants those links to remain visible on Google. but she may object to her reporting being used AI-generated answers that give readers less reason to visit the newspaper itself. What if she cannot refuse the second use without jeopardizing the first? Now consider an independent AI developer building a service for, let's say, Wolof, a language spoken widely in Senegal.
Research has documented how poorly leading language models perform on Wolof and other underrepresented African languages. The developer wants to obtain the permissions she needs, but she cannot afford to negotiate with hundreds of publishers and other rights holders. One wants to restrict access ,, but the systems through which people must exercise those choices.
