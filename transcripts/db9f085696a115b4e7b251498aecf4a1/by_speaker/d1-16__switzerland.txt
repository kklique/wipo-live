I wish you for your attention, Mr. President. Thank you. and perfect timing.
I would like now to give the floor to Ms. Catherine Chammer -Martin, Director General, Swiss Federation of Institute of Intellectual Property. Thank you very much. Switzerland welcomes WIPO's initiative to address artificial intelligence and intellectual property at the highest level.
Norm setting discussions rightly remain with WIPO standing committees such as the SCP and the SCCR. The added value of this Forum lies elsewhere. It brings ministers and heads of IP offices together to discuss the strategic questions that AI raises for our IP systems, for innovation and creativity and for trust. We expect that the exchanges taking place in the Forum will contribute to enrich the discussions in the standing committees and accelerate work where appropriate.
Good policy needs, evidence, and the views of those who use the IP system. This is why the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property and the Intellectual Property Office of Singapore held a joint public consultation on AI and patent law this autumn, covering questions such as inventorship and inventive step.
This cooperation between two offices on two continents shows how we can build a shared understanding. We look forward to sharing the results in the coming months, including with the SCP. Switzerland will host the Geneva AI Summit on the 21 st and 22 nd of June, 2027, under the theme bridging innovation and Trust.
" In the months leading up to the summit, the road to Geneva will bring together governments, business, research and civil society in a series of thematic events and consultations. Any interested party can host such an event and announce it on the summit website. In the same week as the Switzerland and WIPO will hold the IP@AI Summit, a conference dedicated to AI and Intellectual Property.
