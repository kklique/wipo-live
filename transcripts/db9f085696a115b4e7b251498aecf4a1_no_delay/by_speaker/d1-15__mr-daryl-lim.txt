# [d1-15] Protecting What Makes us Human - Is IP the Answer?

# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 16:25-17:25 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 7문장 · 253단어 · 구분 방식: marker

Good morning, good evening. Please imagine with me. a small newspaper. whose readers usually find its reporting through Google search. The publisher wants those links to remain visible on Google. But may object to her reporting being used in AI -generated answers that give readers less reason to visit the newspaper itself. What if she cannot refuse the second use without jeopardizing the first?
Now consider an independent AI developer building a service for, let's say, Wolof, a language spoken widely in Senegal. Research has documented how poorly leading language models perform on Wolof and other underrepresented African languages. The developer wants to obtain the permissions she needs. but she cannot afford to negotiate with hundreds of publishers and other rights holders.
One wants to restrict access.
The other one wants to obtain it. Yet they face a related difficulty. Because copyright choices are shaped not only by what the law permits, but the systems through which people must exercise those choices. So I want to invite us in a few minutes, we have to explore three questions together. First, can right holders meaningfully refuse a use? Second, can developers negotiate permission at a workable cost?
And third, once permission is granted, can anyone actually reliably establish what it covers? So first, take the publisher. and think of two switches.
First, she wants to have search indexing on, and second, a particular AI use of.
If the switches were wired together, refusing the AIUs may mean losing the readers that she depends on.
