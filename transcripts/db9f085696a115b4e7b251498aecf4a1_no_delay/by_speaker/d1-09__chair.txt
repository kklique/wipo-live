Back in 2019, WIPO's technology trends report on artificial intelligence. intelligence was already telling a rather different story. Scientific publications and AI had been growing rapidly for decades. and patent applications were following a similarly dramatic trajectory. with exponential growth in the years leading up to the report. The signals were there, but the question was were we paying attention to them?
And that raises a very interesting possibility. If patent and scientific data could have helped us anticipate the AI revolution, what might they be telling us about the next breakthrough technology? Our next insight, painting by numbers, the power of IP data explores how IP information can offer just a record of past innovation, but a window into its future.
