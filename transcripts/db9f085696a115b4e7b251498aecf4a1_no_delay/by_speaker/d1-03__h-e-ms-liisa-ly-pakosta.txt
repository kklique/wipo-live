of technology and development. Thank you very much. General Excellencies, dear colleagues and friends, thank you for the opportunity to be with you today virtually and to share Estonia's message.
And thank you, WIPO, to bring us all together to discuss this very urgent and important topic. AI and Intellectual Property. We share responsibility. to solve the things in advance, ongoingly and not to do it as only after math. We all agree, we are already living at an AI era. and this means that solving problems creating things, images, texts, whatever looks like differently today than it was yesterday. Intellectual property must be an integral part of AI conversations.
It must be in the middle of it and I would like to underline ahead of it. because people must remain in the center. It is the human mind, the human creator, who must get the profit from the project he or she is managing. It means that we have to debate about authorship and inventorship in the way that there is a clear line in between the instrument and the human mind behind it.
And this means clearly that the creator's rights are in fair balance between protection and access to knowledge. In Estonia we have AI Leap at schools.
It means that teachers and pupils with academia test the use and creation with AI tools while they are studying. They also create new ways of studying throughout the AI era and possibilities. But this is also a very good learning tool. I very much believe that from very early age, kids need education on their Intellectual Property, Intellectual Property in more general, but also AI and Intellectual property.
The third topic I would like to touch is the access to AI tools. AI has already such an impact globally that we should consider it similarly to access to water. It means that especially small and medium-sized enterprises or creators on their own should need open source possibilities to create using the best that humankind has to offer with AI. Otherwise, the existing digital divide will grow very fastly and this would be the result we not be happy about.
It means we have to avoid it definitely. And it is WIPO. to offer here a global platform for discussions, global platform how to avoid digital divide, how to protect creators' rights, but also how to innovate and support innovation in all these areas. It means that everybody has to be included into this dialogue .
It means researchers and creators, politicians and entrepreneurs, but also young people. I mentioned kids. even the kids. Estonia is ready to share our experience and learn from others. Let's build an Intellectual Property system that supports innovation, values, human creativity and ensures that benefits of AI are in our hands but the threats would be avoided. I wish you open and forward looking and productive forum.
