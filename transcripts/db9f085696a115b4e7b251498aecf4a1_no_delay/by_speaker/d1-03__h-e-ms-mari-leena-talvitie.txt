Thank you so much. Dear participants, as the Venice Minister responsible for copyright policy, I am pleased to address this important global forum. We must ensure that continues to support creativity and innovation. in the age of AI. I warmly welcome this idea to bring experts and decision-makers together. We need dialogue, shared understanding and international cooperation. One key area is infrastructure.
Finland has worked on that for many years with stakeholders, public authorities and international partners. Copyright infrastructure should be seen as a simple and natural as a supermarket.
No one questions that products are on by themselves, have and descriptions. The same principle should be applied to creative context. clear and reliable information benefits everyone. This helps. creators and writers to have a better understanding of their work. holders manage their rights list licenses, their works and receive payment for their efforts. It also supports innovation and legal access to content.
Another important issue is the protection of identity. AI can imitate our voices, faces and other personal traits. Non-authorized use harms, perfumers and creators as well as politicians, public figures and ordinary citizens. People must remain in control of how their likeness and identity are used. Clear rules and effective tools are needed. The digital transformation will continue for many years, and no country can address these challenges alone.
