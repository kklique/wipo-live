Natalie, the floor is yours. Good afternoon, everyone. and thank you so very much WIPO for inviting me to moderate this very interesting panel. Today our panelists will include Mr. Anderson Rocha, Ms. Kalpania Tiagi, Mr. Jacob Matheson, Mr. Marco Bassini, and Imogen Heap. And I have to say that there couldn't be a more exciting topic for me today than to talk about what I have been describing as your digital competitive identity.
I've coined this nomenclature, digital competitive identity, to refer to the prospective right that could be acknowledged for all persons today's new technological frontier. and justification for the right is premised on the ineffectiveness perhaps of current IP and related regimes. such as the right to privacy. and personality rights. to provide adequate safeguards for the indicia of an individual's personality.
