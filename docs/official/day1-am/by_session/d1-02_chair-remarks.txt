# 공식 스크립트(WIPO verbatim) · 문서 시각 00:34:35~ · 3단락 · 223단어

I would like to add my welcome to this International Global Forum on Intellectual Property and Artificial Intelligence. Over these two days we will consider how the IP systems can continue to support innovation, creativity and public interest as AI transforms our societies. It is an honor to chair these proceedings. Before we proceed and dive in to the discussions, I have to make a slight adjustment to our program. Due to the unforeseen last minute changes in timing, I will postpone the prerecorded messages from ministers and other officials and in my housekeeping remarks until after the first key presentation.
Perhaps that is a fitting way to begin the Forum on Artificial Intelligence by reminding ourselves how quickly things can change. One of the key questions we have to all face is how to prepare ourselves for the future that is arriving faster than we anticipated. One answer, of course, is to remain flexible and another is better understand what lies ahead. And that is precisely what the first presentation will help us to do. It is my pleasure to welcome Mr. Ren Ito, cofounder and chairman of Sakana AI to share his perspective on what lies ahead.
Mr. Ito, the floor is yours. Thank you so much for the very kind introduction and it is a great honor to be here.
