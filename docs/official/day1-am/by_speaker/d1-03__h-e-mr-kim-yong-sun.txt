# [d1-03] Ministerial-level Remarks
# 발표자: H.E. Mr. Kim Yong Sun  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 10:15-10:45 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 5단락 · 496단어 · 구분 방식: official

I would like to congratulate WIPO on hosting the global forum on IP and AI and thank the Director General and the WIPO team for bringing everyone together to discuss an important issue of the AI era. Although I cannot join you in person, I’m pleased to have this opportunity to share Korea’s experience and policies on this topic. The first is improving IP administration and services through AI. Our ministry is moving towards AI based examination systems using specialized AI models developed in-house. We have already introduced AI into patent searches, trademark classification and design image searches to improve examination quality and we will continue to expand its use to other areas.
To make IP information more accessible to the public, we also plan to introduce an AI powered assistant that explains complex patent documents in plain language. The second is responding to the growing number of AI assisted IP applications. AI has significantly lowered the barriers to filing IP applications. In the first half of 2026, patent filings by individuals in Korea increased by 130% compared to last year. However, as filing becomes easier, we are also seeing a rise in low quality or fraudulent IP applications involving the misuse of AI referred to as IP slop.
To address this problem, our ministry has introduced an early screening system that detects such applications and sends out alerts. We also plan to review our fee system for individual applicants to discourage excessive filings resulting from low application costs. In addition, we will pursue legal and institutional reforms to address the misuse of AI during the application process such as the manipulation of experimental data. These measures will include invalidating patents and imposing financial penalties for such misconduct. And lastly, we are preparing our IP system for the future of AI.
Our ministry will look ahead to the changes AI will bring to the way we work, assess how it will affect our organizational structure and administrative operations and develop strategies accordingly. We also intend to establish the basic principles for AI in intellectual property for AI users and businesses in the IP field to promote responsible AI use and ensure that clear accountability remains with the people. We believe these efforts should go beyond individual countries and become a shared international initiative. Through close cooperation, IP offices can work together to harmonize their systems and develop new international norms in the age of AI. In this spirit, we would like to propose that WIPO take the lead in developing a set of common principles that all Member States can refer to when shaping their IP policies.
AI is bringing a wave of change we have never seen before. The direction we choose to take will determine whether this wave becomes a challenge or an opportunity. I hope starting with this forum, we will work together to find a way forward for IP in the age of AI and make meaningful progress. Thank you very much.
