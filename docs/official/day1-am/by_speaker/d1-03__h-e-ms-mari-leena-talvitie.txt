# [d1-03] Ministerial-level Remarks
# 발표자: H.E. Ms. Mari-Leena Talvitie  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 10:15-10:45 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 4단락 · 224단어 · 구분 방식: official

Dear participants, as minister responsible for copyright policy, I’m pleased to address this important global forum. We must ensure that copyright continues to support creativity and innovation in the age of AI. I warmly welcome this idea to bring experts and decision makers together. We need dialogue, shared understanding and international cooperation. One key area is infrastructure.
Finland has worked on that for many years with stakeholders, public authorities and international partners. Copyright infrastructure should be seen as a symbol and natural as a supermarket. No one questions that products on shelf have prices and descriptions. The same principle should apply to creative content. Clear and reliable information benefits everyone.
This helps creators and rightsholders manage their rights, license their works and receive payment for their efforts. It also supports innovation and legal access to content. Another important issue is the protection of identity. AI can imitate our voices, faces and other personal traits. Nonauthorized use homes and creators as well as politicians, public figures and ordinary citizens.
People must remain in control of how they likeness and identity are used. Clear rules and effective tools are needed. The digital transformation will continue for many years and no country can address these challenges alone. I hope WIPO will continue its strong coordinating role. I wish you productive discussions and every success for this forum.
