This conference represents an important opportunity to strengthen the international dialogue regarding the evolving challenges facing copyright in the age of artificial intelligence and digital transformation.
Ladies and gentlemen, a transformation that is reshaping the ways we produce knowledge, develop innovations, create works and distribute them. While this transformation offers exceptional opportunity, it also raises complex questions regarding the protection of Intellectual Property rights and the use of protected works in training artificial intelligence systems and the attribution of AI generated outputs to their creators and determining their ownership. The challenge we face today is not a choice between protecting Intellectual Property and advancing artificial intelligence. It is about establishing a balanced framework, one that allows both to thrive and in this way we must ensure that technological progress respects legitimate rights and provides appropriate incentives for human creativity and innovation while encouraging responsible access to knowledge and technology. For Egypt, these issues are closely linked.
They are part of our national development priorities. The National Intellectual Property Strategy launched by Egypt in September 2022 along with the establishment of the Egyptian Intellectual Property Authority has laid the foundation for developing a more integrated and modern system and effective one. We are working within this framework. We are working to strengthen institutional governance and update the legislative framework and to increase the contribution of Intellectual Property to economic growth. We are also focusing on developing the creative industries.
We recognize that artificial intelligence calls for a reevaluation of some assumptions that underpin the existing Intellectual Property frameworks. Now, this issue should be based on evidence and thorough consultation and on a clear understanding of the interests of creators and researchers as well as innovators and technology developers. In fact, it should involve society as a whole. At the international level, we all agree that no single country can face these challenges alone. Therefore, we attach great importance to WIPO’s role in facilitating dialogue and the exchange of national expertise and identifying the areas where international cooperation can yield practical results.
It is equally important that developing countries have a significant voice in shaping emerging trends. These transformations should not lead to a widening of the technological gap and the existing economic disparities. Rather, we must ensure that artificial intelligence enables countries to develop their innovative capabilities and strengthen their creative industries.
