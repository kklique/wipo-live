And I’m not here to say that we need a new right because not everything that has a value needs to be protected by IP. There is other ways we can achieve that. That needs to be assessed case by case and it may be that you you end up with a combination of patent rights, trade secrets in certain countries by geographical scope, by product, by service, by launch time. All of that needs to be assessed together.
