# [d1-03] Ministerial-level Remarks
# 발표자: Mr. Kasai Yasuyuki — Commissioner, Japan Patent Office, Japan  (speaker)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 10:10-10:45 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 5단락 · 472단어 · 구분 방식: official

Excellencies, Distinguished Delegates, Ladies and Gentlemen, good morning. My name is Yasuyuki Kasai, Commissioner of the Japan Patent Office, JPO. First of all, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Director General Tang and to all those concerned for providing us with a wonderful opportunity. Today, the world is at a major turning point driven by the rapid advancement of AI. In July of this year, the Government of Japan developed Japan’s growth strategy.
In addition to serving as Commissioner of the JPO, I was responsible for its development as a member of the Cabinet Secretariat. In the strategy, we have selected 17 strategic fields with a projected total scale of domestic, public and private investment expected to exceed 373 yen or approximately 2.33 US dollars cumulatively by fiscal year 2040. Within this framework, the AI transformation position adds an accelerator for growth. Accordingly, several core fields essential for this transformation including AI and semiconductors have been prioritized. By the end of the year, we will formulate the Japan’s growth strategy implementation plan which will include the details and amounts of domestic investment over the next five years leading up to around 2030.
While the Government of Japan positioned AI transformation as an accelerator of growth, the JPO is responding to the rapid development of AI by ensuring that our examination practice is future ready as well as reviewing our institutional frameworks. In Japan, patent applications for AI-related inventions stood at approximately 8,000 in 2019 but exceeded 20,000 in 2024. To address the rapid growth of these applications, the JPO launched the AI examination support team in 2021 to provide support for their examination. In addition, the JPO is promoting the use of AI and its operations and released the JPO AI vision this July. The AI vision advocates a human centric approach as one of its key concepts aiming for the responsible use of AI.
Through these ongoing efforts, the JPO has keenly reaffirmed the importance of a deep collaboration with stakeholders and taking into consideration trend in the foreign countries regarding AI. As AI rapidly advances, it is essential for Member States and stakeholders to come together and share diverse experience, insights and challenges in order to ensure that IP administrations and the IP system continue to support global innovation and creativity. This global platform truly responds to the demand of the times by providing a cross-cutting platform for the dialogue on IP and AI. I would like to pay my sincere respect to WIPO for organizing this forum. Unfortunately, I could not join you today.
Instead, Mr. Nunaka, Deputy Commissioner of the JPO is attending on our behalf. Through this forum and other endeavors, the JPO remains fully committed to contributing to the global discussions on AI and Intellectual Property. Thank you very much for your attention.
