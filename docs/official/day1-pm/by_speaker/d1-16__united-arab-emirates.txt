# [d1-16] Floor Interventions from Member States and Observers
# 발표자: UNITED ARAB EMIRATES  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 5단락 · 430단어 · 구분 방식: official

I am pleased to speak today at the first session of the global forum on Intellectual Property and Artificial Intelligence. This forum is organized by the World Intellectual Property Organizations with the aim of creating an international platform for dialogue and exchange of expertise on the impact of artificial intelligence on the Intellectual Property system. It also aims to develop management mechanisms and workflows within their offices and to enhance their services and to anticipate the future of innovation and creativity. In light of these developments, today, artificial intelligence is reshaping the Intellectual Property system at an accelerated pace. This creates new opportunities and poses challenges for IP offices.
Artificial intelligence tools have the ability to develop IP management mechanisms and improve the services provided. Meanwhile, the increasing use of these tools by applicants is changing the volume of demand and its nature. It also imposes new requirements on workflows and procedures within these offices. In the United Arab Emirates, we view artificial intelligence and Intellectual Property as two integrated fields. Each one enhances the other.
Artificial intelligence contributes to the development of the IP system, speeding up its processes and improving its management efficiency. Meanwhile, Intellectual Property provides a supportive framework for protecting innovations and solutions based on AI technologies. And encouraging their development, the UAE has succeeded in building an advanced model for adopting and deploying artificial intelligence across various critical sectors, including Intellectual Property. This has been achieved through investment in digital infrastructure, developing government services, and strengthening scientific research and innovation, attracting specialized talent which reflects the country’s transition from a stage of adopting modern technologies in the field of Intellectual Property to contributing to the development and production of innovative future solutions that enhance its competitiveness and global standing. This progress, Mr. President, reflects the country’s leadership in adopting artificial intelligence technologies.
It has ranked first globally with a penetration rate of 70.1% among the population in the year. 2026 for the working age population. This is according to the Microsoft AI adoption report for Q1, 2026. In this context, Mr. President, allow me to highlight the UAE’s key efforts in leveraging artificial intelligence technologies and advanced technological solutions. The establishment of the office at the Ministry of Economy based on the latest AI technologies and solutions is a prime example.
This includes the deployment of smart solutions and AI technologies as well as the launch of initiatives such as nest block and IP support among other partnerships. In conclusion, our participation today represents a genuine opportunity to strengthen international cooperation between Intellectual Property offices.
