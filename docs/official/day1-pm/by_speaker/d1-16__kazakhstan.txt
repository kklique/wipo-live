# 발표자: KAZAKHSTAN  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 7단락 · 385단어 · 구분 방식: official

Yes, thank you, Mr. Chair. Distinguished colleagues, okay today we heard many times that AI should keep the human at the center. I would like to share a practical example of how we try to do this in Kazakhstan. As the national Intellectual Property office, we are steadily developing our digital systems. We have automated many of our services and we already use AI in the examination of trademarks, inventions and other IP applications.
It helps our experts work faster and more consistently. But AI is also working for us in copyright. Kazakhstan has launched a unified digital platform for copyright. It is one of the places where creators register their works, users obtain licenses and royalties are collected and distributed. For many years, the main problem in music, royalties was simple - nobody knew exactly what was played.
Distribution was based on incomplete reports and estimates. In practice, this means that well known works were over counted and local and less famous authors were often not counted at all. AI helps us change this in three ways. First, music recognition. Each recording has a unique digital fingerprint.
The system listens to broadcasts, recognizes each track and records what was played where and when. The second is automated monitoring. Today, the platform monitors more than 130 TV and radio channels around the clock. And from the beginning of of 2027, it will cover more than 1,000 users across Kazakhstan. It compares what was actually broadcast with what users reported and shows any gaps.
And third, a clean data. AI helps link each recording to the right holders using international identifiers for recording and musical works together with audio fingerprints. The result is fair distribution. Royalties are based on real use, not on estimates. If a song was played 1,000 times, its author and performers are paid for 1,000 plays.
A young artist from a small town counted in the same way as a major stars. For creators, it means trust for users, clear and predictable rules for the states and more transparent market. We see this as AI in action, not replacing people but making sure that the people behind creative works receive what they have earned. We are open to sharing our experience with other Member States and with WIPO. Thank you.
Thank you.
