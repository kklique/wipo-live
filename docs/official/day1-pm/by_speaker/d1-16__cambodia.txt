Your mic is not on. Thank you, Chair. At the outset, Camero would like to express its sincere appreciation to WIPO for organizing this important forum which provides an excellent opportunity to exchange experience from policymakers, AI developers, heads of IP academy and explore how AI can strengthen IP system worldwide. AI is a rapid transforming the global innovation landscape. It presents a tremendous opportunity to improve efficiency, accessibility and quality of IP services.
At the same time, it raises important questions concerning IP protection, ownership, transparency and responsibility, responsible use of emerging technology. For LDC such as Cambodia, AI represents not only a technological advancement but also an opportunity to accelerate digital transformation, strengthening institutional capacity and support innovation driven economic development. In this regard, Cambodia, through its Department of IP under the Ministry of Commerce is working closely with WIPO to modernize its IP administration. Our future IP services aim to introduce AI supported trademark classification, similarity, search, custom assistance, simplify online filing and improve data analytics. This initiative intended to make IP services more efficient, transparent and accessible and user friendly, particularly for small and medium size enterprises.
We also explore modernized GI administration through digital registry solution to support the protection of promoting our traditional product and cultural heritage. In this connection, Cambodia wishes to emphasize three priorities, first, strengthening technical assistance and capacity building to enable developing country to adopt AI responsibly in their IP administration. Second, promote inclusive access to AI technology and digital tool, particularly for least developing country and small and medium enterprise. Third, ensuring international IP framework continue to involve in balanced manner, encouraging AI driven innovation while safeguarding human creativity rights and public interests. Last but not least, we believe that AI should serve a bridge to greater opportunity rather than create a wider digital divide between developed and developing country.
