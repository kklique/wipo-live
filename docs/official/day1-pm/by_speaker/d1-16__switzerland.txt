# [d1-16] Floor Interventions from Member States and Observers
# 발표자: SWITZERLAND  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 4단락 · 299단어 · 구분 방식: official

Switzerland welcomes WIPO’s initiative to address artificial intelligence and Intellectual Property at the highest level. Non-setting discussions rightly remain with WIPO Standing Committees such as the SCP and the SCCR. The added value of this forum lies elsewhere. It brings ministers and heads of IP offices together to discuss the strategic questions that AI raises for our IP systems for innovation and creativity and for trust. We expect that the exchanges taking place in the forum will contribute to enrich the discussions in the Standing Committees and accelerate work where appropriate.
Good policy needs, evidence and the views of those who use the IP system. This is why the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property and the Intellectual Property Office of Singapore held a joint public consultation on AI and patent law this autumn covering questions such as inventorship and inventive step. This cooperation between two offices on two continents shows how we can build a shared understanding. We look forward to sharing the results in the coming months including with the SCP. Switzerland will host the Geneva AI Summit on the 21st and 22nd of June, 2027 under the theme bridging innovation and trust.
In the months leading up to the summit, the road to Geneva will bring together governments, business research and civil society in a series of thematic events and consultations. Any interested party can host such an event and announce it on the summit website. In the same week as the summit, Switzerland and WIPO will hold the IP at AI Summit, a conference dedicated to AI and Intellectual Property. I intend to draw on the discussions of these two days to enrich our road to Geneva activities and the IP at AI Summit. We look forward to welcoming you again in Geneva.
Thank you.
