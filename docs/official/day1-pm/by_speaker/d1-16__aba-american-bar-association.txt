It further resolved that such legislation should include strong safeguards to ensure the legislation’s compatibility with our first amendment, free speech rights. And it further resolved that any such legislation should consider its impact on the right of publicity and the right of privacy under state, territorial and tribal law, technological innovation and creations and potential third-party liability. In particular, the IP section lauded a United States bill called the No Fakes Act as an important step towards the development of a national uniform framework to protect an individual’s voice and visual likeness while balancing the public interest and many other related issues at stake. The section hopes that enactment of such legislation in the United States will serve as a valuable tool to support the use of generative AI while addressing the risks and perils of generative AI. We would welcome the opportunity to work with any of the Member States, with WIPO, other observers or other stakeholders.
