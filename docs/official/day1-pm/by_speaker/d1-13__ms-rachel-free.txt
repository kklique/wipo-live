The intervenors made the case for greater harmonization between the European Patent Office and the UK law. And many of those suggestions in the intervention were followed by the Supreme Court. The Supreme Court set out a new test, any hardware test followed by an intermediate filtering step and then the UK holistic test for inventive step. Next slide, please. So what are the implications of this?
Well, it’s a better commercial situation for applicants. Having greater harmonization with the European Patent Office law is a much better commercial situation. Also, it used to be the case that the UK IPO would issue a no search decision in a situation where the patent application was directed to material that was thought to be excluded. And that practice has now ended. So this gives a much better commercial situation.
But in the short term, we have some legal uncertainty. And that’s because the Supreme Court left many questions unanswered. One of those questions is how to apply the intermediate filtering step together with the UK holistic test for inventive step. Another of those questions concerns technical implementations. Those are something that is used by the European Patent Office to enable patents for core AI technology to be granted.
It is currently uncertain whether those will apply in the UK as well or not. Another implication is drift correction. This decision has corrected the drift between the European Patent Office law and the UK law rather than giving a permanent alignment. So drift might happen again over the years. The last implication I have got is that it throws in question the EPO problem and solution test.
And that’s because the UK is using a holistic approach to assess inventive step. And so is the unified patent court. Whereas in contrast, the European Patent Office assesses a patent application using the problem and solution test. So it seems odd to have different tests for granting a patent than for litigating a patent. Having said that, the EPO problem and solution approach gives very consistent high quality outcomes and great legal certainty.
