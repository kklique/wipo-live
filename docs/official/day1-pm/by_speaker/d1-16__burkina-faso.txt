It is dedicated to the opportunities and challenges posed by artificial intelligence for innovation, creativity and Intellectual Property systems. Aware of these issues, my country has launched several initiatives aimed at promoting a sovereign digital transformation that is inclusive and responsible. Thank rise of AI-related technologies, Burkina Faso remains convinced that AI policies must fully incorporate considerations related to Intellectual Property in order to ensure a balance between promoting innovation, protecting creators, and ensuring equitable access to knowledge and technology. With this in mind, my country, through its competent national structures, is continuing its efforts notably through the national center for Intellectual Property and the office of Burkina Faso copyright awareness raising and promotion activities for Intellectual Property in collaboration with WIPO.
In this vein, the BBDA has dedicated the 7th edition of the copyright back to school event to 2025, the challenges of collective management in the age of AI. These initiatives demonstrate the attention given to adapting protection mechanisms to technological changes. Burkina Faso also considers it essential to preserve the human creativity and to strengthen the protection of rights of individuals against the abusive use of their image, their voice, and their creations by AI. To this end, my country encourages the WIPO to intensify its support for Developing Countries, in particular in terms of capacity building, access to technological data, and the development of appropriate legal frameworks. To conclude, we reaffirm Burkina Faso’s commitment to equitable and inclusive AI governance that respects the rights of creators and is firmly centered on human dignity.
We hope that the discussions and conclusions from this forum will meet the expectations of Member States. Thank you. Thank you.
