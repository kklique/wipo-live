# 발표자: IAWG (International Affiliation of Writers’ Guilds)  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 3단락 · 266단어 · 구분 방식: official

I speak on behalf of the International Affiliation of Writers Guild. My name is Jennifer Davidson. We are a global organization that represents some 64,000 screenwriters worldwide. I also speak as a working screenwriter myself who is practical understanding of the implications of what we are discussing here today. We gratefully acknowledge the time and consideration all parties have put into organizing this global forum.
It is no secret that our members and creators worldwide reject opt-out approaches as a solution to the nonconsensual use of our works by AI companies to train their models. When we talk about consent as a principle in all aspects of life, we understand that it must be sought repeatedly and enthusiastically. It cannot just be assumed. To be asked and to have the ability to refuse is central to this debate and it is clear that creators have not given our repeated and enthusiastic consent for the theft of our work and yet we continue to be told that we can just opt out as though that is a solution. Robots.text is inadequate in too many ways to go into in three minutes but I do want to highlight that it may have the further consequence of eliminating the discoverability of an artist’s online presence through Google’s AI search results.
So if we are truly seeking ways to move forward today, perhaps we can start with agreeing to abandon ideas of opting out as a workable model for gaining consent and also that we stop talking about artists’ work in the same way that we talk about scientific data.
