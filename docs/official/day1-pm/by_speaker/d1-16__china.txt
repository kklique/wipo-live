# [d1-16] Floor Interventions from Member States and Observers
# 발표자: CHINA  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 9단락 · 568단어 · 구분 방식: official

Artificial intelligence, dear Director General, Delegates, the global wave of intelligence is rising. Artificial intelligence technology has entered an unprecedented period of vitality. It is releasing tremendous energy. It is reshaping the way humans create. It is transforming the landscape of communication and the knowledge ecosystem, the world Intellectual Property Organization is hosting the global forum on Intellectual Property and Artificial Intelligence.
The timing could not be more opportune. It is of great significance. Chinese President Xi Jinping has profoundly pointed out that artificial intelligence should be an international public good that benefits all of humanity. China is willing to work with countries around the world to carry out extensive international cooperation in the field of artificial intelligence. We will strengthen cooperation on development strategies, governance rules and technical standards.
We will also enhance alignment and coordination in these areas. This will promote the healthy and vigorous development of the intelligent industry. We will ensure that the benefits of intelligence better reach the people of all countries. In recent years, China has put forward the global initiative on AI governance and the AI capacity building inclusive program. We have also launched the AI plus international cooperation initiative.
We have a established the world organization for AI cooperation. We are working to achieve a global action plan for AI governance. China adheres to the philosophy of putting people first and using intelligence for good. We advocate for the equal development of all countries and the equitable use of artificial intelligence. We are committed to helping countries in the global south bridge the digital divide.
This provides important guidance for the copyright system in addressing the challenges posed by artificial intelligence. With regard to global governance of AI copyright, China puts forward the following propositions. First, we must uphold the value principle of putting people first. No matter how artificial intelligence technology evolves, human creative labor will always be the source of cultural prosperity. The copyright system should continue to incentivize and protect human creativity.
We must prevent AI generated content from replacing human civilization and from undermining or eroding it. Second, we must adhere to a prudent and inclusive institutional approach. The copyright system has a dual mission, to incentivize creation and to promote dissemination. China is willing to work with all parties to address issues such as the licensing of training data, the ownership of generated content and liability for infringement, including the allocation of responsibility and mechanisms for sharing benefits. We will explore solutions that balance innovation with fairness.
Third, we must uphold a rule-based system of collaborative governance. China insists on the coordinated advancement of legislation, justice and law enforcement. We adhere to a multistakeholder governance model involving governments, businesses, society and the international community. We are building a diversified mechanism, a comprehensive governance framework that operates in parallel. It covers the pre-event, in-event and post-event phases, ensuring all-round coverage.
We will continue to improve the copyright rules for artificial intelligence. Fourth, we must firmly commit to the direction of multilateral cooperation. China supports the World Intellectual Property Organization in playing its role as a platform. We will promote the establishment of international rules that enjoy broad consensus. We will strengthen capacity building for developing countries and provide support.
Let the Intellectual Property system become a bridge for promoting the global development of artificial intelligence and a link. Finally, I wish this forum a complete success.
