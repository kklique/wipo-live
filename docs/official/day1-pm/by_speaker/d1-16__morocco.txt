# 발표자: MOROCCO  (floor)
# Day 1 · 2026-11-09 · 예정 17:25-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 4단락 · 425단어 · 구분 방식: official

Hello, everyone. Mr. President, ladies and gentlemen, first of all, I would like to congratulate Mr. Daren Tang for organizing this global forum on Intellectual Property and Artificial Intelligence. It is a crucial gathering for the Intellectual Property community and it offers us a privileged space for dialogue, for reflection and cooperation. In this context of major technological transformation and the profound changes brought about by artificial intelligence, it is essential to preserve the central role of the inventor and human creativity. This remains an imperative to stimulate innovation and ensure a balance between protection and creation and legal certainty.
Furthermore, artificial intelligence is now emerging as an important lever for Industrial Property offices and it allows for the improvement of procedures, the automation of management and the streamlining of internal processes. This is to meet the demands for speed and efficiency. Morocco has made the strategic choice to place AI at the heart of its new development model by focusing on innovation, the development of technological infrastructure and human capital. As pillars for transforming its economy, this is a strong testament to this national priority at the highest level. The Moroccan government has recently established a ministry of artificial intelligence.
Within this same national and international dynamic dynamic, WIPO is continuing its digital transformation by deploying increasingly high performing digital platforms. These platforms are accessible and focused on the needs of stakeholders of the IP ecosystem. Building on these achievements and as part of the development of the 2027 to 2035 strategic vision for Industrial Property our priority is to make AI a major lever for operational excellence. We plan to continue the gradual integration of AI-based solutions, in particular to offer simplified processes and a better customer experience, to improve the work of examiners and the tools available to them and to provide services that meet the expectations of the various stakeholders. Morocco reaffirms its full commitments to contribute alongside the UNHCR and its partners to initiatives aimed at supporting IP offices and stakeholders in seizing the opportunities offered by AI as well as in adapting our systems and practices to address legal challenges and technical challenges posed by artificial intelligence.
It is also our responsibility in a spirit of constructive collaboration to ensure that the integration of advanced technologies into industrial and Intellectual Property systems takes into account the realities of different economies. In order to effectively support the development of our countries, I wish every success to the work of this forum and I thank you for your attention, Mr. President.
