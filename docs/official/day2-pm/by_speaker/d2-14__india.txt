# [d2-14] Floor Interventions from Member States and Observers
# 발표자: INDIA  (floor)
# Day 2 · 2026-11-10 · 예정 17:15-17:50 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 3단락 · 245단어 · 구분 방식: official

For these two days, we heard important perspective on IP offices, authorship and inventorship and the interplay between training, data and models. Our takeaways are these. One, on IP administration, there is a shared interest in responsible AI use with human oversight, explainability and security. In helping smaller offices keep pace, India supports WIPO developing best practices and capacity building programs and offers to share its own experience. Two, on authorship and inventorship, the principle of meaningful human contribution commands wide support.
Different jurisdictions are at different stages and a comparative study and shared principles would be valuable. I would like to suggest that how do you protect the IP rights of the indigenous and traditional knowledge is also an important aspect. Three, on training data and models, no single approach has all the answers. Fair demonstration, workable access, transparency and open innovation must be considered together. Four, on the process itself, we request that the Secretariat, A, prepare a summary of the Forum’s discussions and Member States’ proposals and, two, consult Member States on the establishment of an observatory, a copyright and data dialogue and set of voluntary principles and, C, confirm the Forum as an annual event with space for Member States’ presentation of national share their national experiences.
With that, I would like to thank once again all the organizers and WIPO especially for this event and we look forward for meaningful engagement with WIPO as a member. Thank you very much.
