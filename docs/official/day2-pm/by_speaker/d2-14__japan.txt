# [d2-14] Floor Interventions from Member States and Observers
# 발표자: JAPAN  (floor)
# Day 2 · 2026-11-10 · 예정 17:15-17:50 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 3단락 · 367단어 · 구분 방식: official

The impact of AI is reaching every aspect of our economy and society and the pace of change continues to accelerate. The scale of this transformation is presenting significant challenges as well as opportunities for the Intellectual Property system. We had many perspectives on a wide range of issues, how to promote both the protection and the use of data needed for AI, how to balance AI development and adoption with the fair distribution of its benefits, how differing national interests and stakeholder perspectives can be reconciled and even how AI may fundamentally reshape the principles of the IP system and the role of IP offices themselves. While it may be difficult to reach a common understanding on all of these issues today, I believe it is a very important achievement that the key challenges have been comprehensively identified and that such valuable experiences and insights have been shared at the high level. At the same time, as I would like to emphasize one point, AI development is not only rapid, it is accelerating.
What we consider a common understanding today may no longer be a common understanding tomorrow. Therefore, to keep pace with these rapid developments, we must continue to examine with a sense of urgency how the IP system should evolve to support global innovation and creativity and how IP offices should adapt to this changing environment. Director General Tan reminded us that this may no longer be a moment for safe conversations, for wait and see or business as usual. I share that view. As human beings, we naturally tend to prefer familiar approaches and established practices.
That is the reason why strong leadership and high level commitment to change are essential. In that regard, I believe this global forum has provided an excellent opportunity for all of us to recognize not only the need for change but also the need to begin that change today. Finally, as AI continues to evolve, I hope that WIPO will continue to provide opportunities like this forum so that we can learn from one another, work together, and respond to further development in a coordinated and timely manner. I thank you very much. Thank you very much.
