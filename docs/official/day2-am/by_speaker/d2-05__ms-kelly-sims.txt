# [d2-05] Insight: Efficiency in Action - Deploying AI in IP Administration
# 발표자: Ms. Kelly Sims — Acting Deputy Director General, IP Australia, Australia  (speaker)
# Day 2 · 2026-11-10 · 예정 11:30-11:50 (Geneva) · 발언 2회 · 7단락 · 684단어 · 구분 방식: official

Hi, everyone. At IP Australia our focus is ensuring the IP system continues to support innovation in an AI driven economy while still maintaining trust, transparency and confidence in IP rights. We also see lots of potential as we are hearing across the last two days for AI to improve our own business and our own customer experience as well. Innovation is one of the four strategic objectives in our corporate plan and an innovative mindset is a key pillar of our people strategy. But that doesn’t mean deploying AI for the sake of it.
We look at ways of working. We look at capability. We look at efficiency and technology together. Rather than treating AI as a stand alone technology project. Our direction is to move from individuals experimenting with AI towards AI becoming a strategic organizational capability that improves our services, improves regulatory outcomes and also improves our operational efficiency.
Scaling doesn’t mean you adopt everything. We want to experiment safely. We want to scale what delivers clear benefits to our agency and we want to stop or redesign what doesn’t work. And I can understand some of the things that Rahul was just saying there about continuous feedback loops. We use targeted AI capabilities to support classification, workflow, decision support as well as customer facing tools that are already out there in the market such as TM checker and also IP first response which you can find on our website.
We are also encouraging staff to build confidence using approved generative AI tools for their day-to-day work and two examples I wanted to share with you on efficiency that we are working on. In trademarks we are developing an AI assisted drafting of further exam reports to improve consistency, quality and efficiency. AI produces the draft but the examiner remains responsible for the final report and the decision. We began that experiment with mock customer submissions, so ones we kind of made up ourselves just to test the technology and the way people interacted but we are now testing with real customer files in that experiment. The second one in patents, we are building on our existing AI family member analyzer to help examiners access and interrogate information from other IP offices.
Following two waves of experimentation and a successful proof of concept, we are now commencing the production build of that tool into our 400 strong pool of examiners, a little less than my colleagues from China, but still a big part of our team. We have adopted a business led product management approach, we talked about that as well where we are starting with customer staff and policy needs to define the outcomes we want and direct resources to investments that are going to create the most values. We heard a lot in the last panel about people and adoption is fundamentally a people and change challenge in addition to having the technology and I guess perhaps what I will finish with, Ken, in my input here is in addition to good training, bringing your customers with you on the journey, we have done a lot of testing with our customers right from the beginning and that builds trust because we use their feedback to iterate our tools and make them better. The other aspect would be good governance, good governance enables innovation, it doesn’t strategically block it. What you want to do is to make sure that you are scaling and with having appropriate governance and oversight but not actually dampening people’s innovative spirit in order to kind of bring the innovation you need.
Successful innovation is done with people, not to people. That is our experience and we in IP Australia we talk about people plus technology is the way we want to drive our future and kind of serve our customers and I guess that’s our ultimate destination, Ken, skilled people equipped with better tools delivering trusted IP services for our customers and for our government. Thank you very much, Kelly.
