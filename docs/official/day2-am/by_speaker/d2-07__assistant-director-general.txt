That was a fantastic and very lively discussions. For our final AI in action showcases we are taking you to Guinea where a young innovator is tackling a major public health challenge. In many communities diagnosing diseases such as malaria and tuberculosis remains difficult, particularly where laboratories and specialists expertise are scarce. But what if AI could bring diagnostic capabilities closer to the people who need them? Our next speaker, Mr. Alain Ephraim Gomu, a young innovator and entrepreneur from Guinea is developing an AI-powered diagnostic device designed to detect these diseases rapidly even without an Internet connection.
Alain, floor is yours.
