Thank you for having me. When I think about AI in the center or the human in the center, as an economic expert, I think about two things. I think about value and risk. From a question of value, it is largely about apportionment. What was the contribution of the AI as opposed to the human?
AI is very good at generating many ideas but it is the enablement that really has the value showing that it can be implemented for success. And then with respect to risk, that largely is a question about governance. So we focus on emerging tools like governance ratings. We utilize the AIQ score as one measure to understand what is the contribution in risk of AI as opposed to the human which is enabling the solution.

At Ocean Tomo our work is squarely at the forefront of those valuation questions. I think the discussion today about developing markets and accountability speaks directly to the notion of value and risk. On the one hand we are still waiting for the courts to clearly define what is fair use and what is not fair use with respect to these AI models. We anticipate that question will be answered in the coming months or certainly the coming year or two. With respect to how we are looking at those issues internally, one of the key parameters for us is timing, that the speed at which technology is developing is shortening and so whether or not the traditional patenting model, for example, is appropriate is being reassessed by the time the patent issued has the technology moved on.
So what we are finding is that there is greater attention to trade secret protection, copyright protection as opposed to the traditional patent point of view. The other thing that I would just emphasize is it all goes back to that question of apportionment, whether it be film or whether it be authored content, how much was fairly attributable to the AI as opposed to the human interaction and we are now focusing more on the enablement and in particular with respect to patents as well, the public agreement is that disclosure provides you a period of exclusivity. Well, the disclosure of a random idea is efficient with AI but building the technology, enabling the technology and describing it in a way that future generations can leverage it is really where the value is shifting and that’s been our focus. Well, thank you so much. You have lighted the importance of the fair use, timing and proportionality.
