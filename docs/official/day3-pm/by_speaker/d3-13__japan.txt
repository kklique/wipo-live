# [d3-13] Floor Interventions from Member States, Observers, and Members of the Technical Exchange Network
# 발표자: JAPAN  (floor)
# Day 3 · 2026-11-11 · 예정 17:30-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 6단락 · 425단어 · 구분 방식: official

The AITARA initiative led by WIPO and the holding of today’s first annual meeting are truly appreciated. Japan in the face of rapid advancements in AI has focused on the technical and practical aspects of the Intellectual Property System and has created a platform for international dialogue. We highly commend WIPO’s strong initiative. The Japanese government will host in August 2026 the Osaka principles on the protection and transparency of Intellectual Property for the appropriate use of generative AI. These principles have been formulated.
They require generative AI providers to ensure transparency of training data, implement measures to protect Intellectual Property rights and respond to information requests from rights holders. This is a comply or explain approach. As a voluntary framework without legal binding force it aims to balance the development of AI technology with the protection of Intellectual Property rights. What is being discussed at ATRIPROF includes technical infrastructures such as digital watermarks, content authentication and rights management systems. These are the technical measures required by Japan’s principle code to prove the origin and history of content such as digital watermarks and C2Pascals.
Ensuring the transparency of crawlers are also part of these efforts. Their directions are aligned and the two are complementary to each other. We recognize them as such. The international reference indicators or benchmarks for AI-related technologies that are expected as a result of ATRIPROF will be utilized in the efforts of various countries, including Japan. This will enhance the consistency of international technology.
On the other hand, by feeding back the practical experience gained through Japan’s principle code to a 3/3 we expect a positive feedback loop in both directions. In addition, the principle code also stipulates how to respond to requests for disclosure of information regarding learning data from both rights holders and users. The development of content authentication and provenance verification technologies promoted by AIII will strengthen the technical basis for such disclosure requests. For rights holders it will enhance the effectiveness of protection. We believe that efforts from both the technological and institutional aspects are essential for protecting the rights of creators.
Japan believes that building a technical infrastructure that aligns with the actual technological and practical realities is indispensable for the proper functioning of Intellectual Property rights in the AI era. We are convinced of this. Japan places great importance on a 3I as a forum for technical knowledge exchange, distinct from policy discussions. We will continue to collaborate with experts in industry and contribute constructively to the A3I dialogue. Thank you.
Thank you very much.
