# [d3-13] Floor Interventions from Member States, Observers, and Members of the Technical Exchange Network
# 발표자: REPUBLIC OF KOREA  (floor)
# Day 3 · 2026-11-11 · 예정 17:30-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 3단락 · 228단어 · 구분 방식: official

We also wish to thank the technical expert network members and speakers who shared their valuable expertise and insights on diverse cross-cutting issues concerning artificial intelligence infrastructure relevant to copyright. The Republic of Korea highly commands WIPO’s global initiative in establishing a timely forum, bringing together private sector experts to synthesize discussions on pioneering AI-related copyright infrastructure. In a constructive effort to facilitate the future work of this valuable forum, the Republic of Korea would like to propose two suggestions. First, we believe that we need discussions that look beyond traditional content genres such as music, film or literary works. Instead, our discussion needs to extend to identifying the specific contribution of each work integrated into multimodal content and linking that information to accurate compensation for rights holders.
Second, we believe that pursuing greater consistency in schematization across industry sectors will be beneficial for driving future discussion under the IIII, ultimately facilitating the utilization of their outcomes. We also hope that the WIPO Secretariat will provide Member States with an opportunity to share their respective industrial landscape, ensuring such insights are reflected when mapping out industry workflows. The Republic of Korea looks forward to diverse discussions to take place within IIII. We remain fully committed to supporting private sector experts and actively contributing to future discussion in a constructive spirit. Thank you very much.
