# 발표자: IFRRO (International Federation of Reproduction Rights Organizations)  (floor)
# Day 3 · 2026-11-11 · 예정 17:30-17:55 (Geneva) · 발언 1회 · 2단락 · 212단어 · 구분 방식: official

Thank you, Mr. Chair. Speaking on behalf of IFRRO, IFRRO welcomes this first annual meeting of the AI infrastructure interchange and appreciates WIPO’s efforts to facilitate technical exchanges and cooperation among the different sectors. The work undertaken through the technical exchange network across its four focus groups on industry workflows, metadata framing, AI generated content taxonomy and a common lexicon provides a useful opportunity to better understand existing infrastructures and identify common challenges. IFRRO is pleased to contribute to this initiative, particularly through the metadata framing focus group bringing the practical experience of reproduction rights organizations in rights identification, information management, licensing and cross-border exchanges. As the initiative moves forward we would encourage WIPO to maintain a strong focus on the practical needs of the different sectors ensuring that the work of the four focus groups remains connected and that their findings can inform concrete next steps.
In doing so, we believe it is important to build upon existing expertise and take account of the diversity of systems and practices across sectors. IFRRO looks forward to continuing its constructive participation in the TEN. Thank you. Thank you very much.
